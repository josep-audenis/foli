Master services agreement

This agreement is made between Northwind Traders Limited, the Customer, and Fabrikam Consulting LLP, the Supplier, and takes effect on the date of the last signature.

1. Services

The Supplier shall design and implement the Customer's data platform, train its staff and provide second-line support during business hours. Each deliverable is accepted in writing before the next one starts.

The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, and in accordance with good industry practice.

2. Fees and payment

The Customer shall pay the fees set out in the schedule. Invoices are payable within thirty (30) forty-five (45) days of receipt.

Late payments carry interest at the statutory rate, calculated daily from the due date until the invoice is paid in full.

3. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secure, use it only to perform this agreement, and disclose it only to staff who need to know it.

These duties continue for five years after the agreement ends, and do not cover information that is already public or that the law requires a party to disclose.

4. Intellectual property

Each party keeps the rights it held before this agreement. On payment in full, the Customer owns the deliverables made for it under this agreement.

5. Liability

Neither party limits liability for death, personal injury or fraud. Otherwise each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the preceding twelve months.

6. Termination

Either party may end this agreement on ninety days' written notice, or at once if the other commits a material breach and does not remedy it within thirty days.

Foli puts every line on a real page during editing. Documents follow Word's model, so a contract keeps its styles, numbering and tracked changes.

9 test files have been compared with Word: 147 of 155 paragraphs and 277 of 280 table-cell paragraphs start on the same page. The comparison used Word's fonts and predates headers and footers.

Footnotes are not shown yet. Headers and footers arrive in 0.6.

Save a .docx and the rest of the file stays put.

9 test files have been saved without edits. Only word/document.xml changed in each one. Every other part was copied byte for byte.

Settle a draft, all at once one change at a time.

With tracking on, each edit is saved under its author's name. Changes and comments open in Word.

Long tables cross pages.

The header row repeats on the next page. Merged cells, table styles and tracked rows are supported.

These sample rows stay whole. Rows can also break across pages.

An engine built from scratch.

No ProseMirror, Tiptap, Lexical or Slate underneath. Foli owns the layout, so the caret and selection cross page breaks like any other line.

Add it to a React app.

FoliEditor includes pages, a toolbar and comments. Documents are JSON and can be stored anywhere. The guide covers custom toolbars, Next.js and using Foli without React.

npm i @folijs/react @folijs/core @folijs/docx
Editor.tsx
"use client";
import { createDocument } from "@folijs/core";
import { FoliEditor } from "@folijs/react";
import "@folijs/react/styles.css";

const initial = createDocument();

export function Editor() {
  return <FoliEditor initialContent={initial} />;
}
