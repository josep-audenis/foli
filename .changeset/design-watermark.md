Watermarks (Design ▸ Watermark): Word's text and picture watermarks read from headers (an
opaque run's `watermark`, schema 9), drawn behind the text of every page, written back as Word
writes them; `setWatermark` and `watermarkOf` in core; `toolbarItems.watermark` (Word's presets,
Custom watermark…, Remove) and its dialog in the Design tab. New strings: "Watermark", "Custom
watermark…", "Remove watermark", the presets' and the dialog's.
