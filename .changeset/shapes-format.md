Word's Shape Format tab for a selected shape or text box: shape styles, fill, outline color,
weight, dashes and arrows (`setShapeLook` in core; `toolbarItems.shapeStyles`, `shapeFill`,
`shapeOutline`, `outlineWeight`, `outlineDashes`, `outlineArrows`). `@folijs/docx` writes a
shape's text color into its style.
