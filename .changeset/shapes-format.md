Word's Shape Format tab: fill, outline color and weight for a selected shape or text box, and a
shape's styles, dashes and arrows (a line's) (`setShapeLook` in core; `toolbarItems.shapeStyles`, `shapeFill`,
`shapeOutline`, `outlineWeight`, `outlineDashes`, `outlineArrows`). `@folijs/docx` writes a
shape's text color into its style.
