Crop handles and sizing (33.5): Picture Format ▸ Crop (core's `toggleCrop`, `croppingAt`) puts a
crop handle on each edge of the selected picture, a drag crops it; text boxes get the resize
handle too (their sides follow the pointer, Shift keeps the ratio); `setObjectSize` and the
Layout dialog's Size tab (height, width, scale, lock aspect ratio, rotation), with Size… in
Picture Format.
