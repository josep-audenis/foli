Word's Paragraph dialog: `toolbarItems.paragraphSettings` (the launcher of the Word ribbon's
Paragraph groups), Paragraph… in the right-click menu, and `openDialog("paragraph")` in core to
open it from code. Mixed values show blank and OK applies only what was changed, in one undo
step (`chainCommands`). New toolbar strings for the dialog (`toolbarMessages`).
