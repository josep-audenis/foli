Word's Font group: `toggleVerticalAlign`, `stepFontSize`, `clearFormatting`, `changeCase` and
`setUnderline`, with Word's keys (Ctrl+Shift+=, Ctrl+=, Ctrl+Space, Ctrl+Shift+> and <); `keyName`
names the =, period and comma keys by their place. The Word ribbon has them in Home's Font group.
