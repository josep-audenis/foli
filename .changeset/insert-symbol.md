Insert ▸ Symbol: `insertSymbol(text, font?)` in core (a symbol in the font chosen, as Word writes
it), Word's keys for its special characters (em dash, nonbreaking hyphen and space, ©, ®, ™,
ellipsis), and `toolbarItems.symbol` in React: Word's recent symbols and its Symbol dialog
(`"symbol"`; subsets by font, Special Characters). New toolbar messages: the dialog's labels,
subsets and special characters' names.
