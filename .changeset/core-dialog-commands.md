Commands for Word's Paragraph and Font dialogs: `setIndent`, `setParagraphFlags`,
`setOutlineLevel`, `setCharacterSpacing` and `setRunEffects`; `setLineSpacing` takes Word's line
rule (`"exact"`, `"atLeast"`) as a second argument. `paragraphFormatAt` and `runFormatAt` give
what the whole selection shows, leaving out what it differs in.
