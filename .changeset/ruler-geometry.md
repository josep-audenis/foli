`view.rulerGeometry()` (and `editor.storyView.rulerGeometry`): where a ruler's parts are on the
screen for the caret, in client px at any zoom: its page and margins, its paragraph's indents
and tab stops, and the columns of the table it is in (`RulerGeometry`, in core).
