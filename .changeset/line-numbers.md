`exportPdf`); a paragraph's `suppressLineNumbers` (schema 9) read and written, in
`setParagraphFlags` and the Paragraph dialog; `toolbarItems.lineNumbers` (Layout ▸ Line Numbers
and Word's Line Numbers dialog). New strings: "Line Numbers", "Line numbers", the menu's and the
dialog's, "Formatting exceptions", "Suppress line numbers".
