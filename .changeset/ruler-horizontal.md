`FoliRuler`: Word's horizontal ruler over the caret's page, with its margins shaded, numbers in
inches or centimetres, and the first-line, hanging, left and right indents and the page's left
and right margins as sliders, dragged (one undo step; Alt for exact values; Escape cancels) or
moved with the arrow keys. `view.rulerGeometry` now measures a paragraph during a composition
and takes the gutter of the page's own section.
