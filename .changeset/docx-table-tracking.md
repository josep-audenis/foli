Reads and writes tables', rows' and cells' tracked formatting (`w:tblPrChange`, `w:trPrChange`, `w:tcPrChange`) as core's `formatChange` (schema 8), so Word's records of a table style, a header row or a cell's shading are listed, accepted and rejected in the editor, and the editor's are saved as Word reads them.
