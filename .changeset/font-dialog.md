Word's Font dialog: `toolbarItems.fontSettings` (the Word ribbon's Font group launcher), Font… in
the right-click menu, and Ctrl+D in `wordKeymap()` (`openDialog("font")`). Core adds
`setVerticalAlign`, an underline color in `setUnderline(style, color?)`, and bold and italic in
`setRunEffects`. New toolbar strings for the dialog (`toolbarMessages`).
