The Word ribbon's Layout tab as Word's: Margins, Orientation, Size, Columns and Breaks menus
(`toolbarItems.margins`, `orientation`, `pageSize`, `columns`, `breaks`) and the Indent and
Spacing boxes (`indentLeft`, `indentRight`, `spaceBefore`, `spaceAfter`). The Section menu
leaves the ribbon's Layout tab (it stays in `toolbarItems` and the default toolbar). New toolbar
strings (`toolbarMessages`).
