What cost a model retries: find (the editor's and the agent's) looks through soft hyphens and zero-width spaces and takes a non-breaking hyphen as `-`; an agent refusal quotes invisible and look-alike characters escaped (`‑`) and never cuts a surrogate pair; `read` gives a table cell's grid column, the columns a merged cell spans and a vertical merge, and a table's grid columns. In the MCP server: a result over 200 000 characters is cut at a block's end and keeps its `(more: …)` line; `null` for an argument that may be left out is taken as leaving it out; a save the system refuses (a file open in Word) says so; a request that is not JSON-RPC 2.0 is answered with its id; `console.log` goes to stderr, never into the protocol.
